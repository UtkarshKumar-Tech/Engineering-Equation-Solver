Choosing a method

Bisection

Most dependable when the initial interval brackets a root. Convergence is linear and requires a sign change.

Newton–Raphson

Often the fewest iterations near a simple root, but depends on the initial guess and derivative quality.

Secant

Does not need a derivative and often converges faster than Bisection, though convergence is not guaranteed.
